Pure-Rust MP3 decoding

Audio Transcription Studio decodes MP3 frames via symphonia directly inside the Rust backend, downmixes stereo channels to mono, and resamples to 16 kHz PCM for Whisper.

Podcasts and long MP3 recordings

Voice activity segmentation splits long MP3 files on pauses and keeps continuous chunks under 26 seconds so timestamps remain accurate across 5-minute and 1-hour episodes.

Corrupted MP3 protection

Truncated or invalid MP3 streams are caught during container probing, logged as CORRUPTED_FILE, and isolated from the remaining queue.

podcast.mp3 -> podcast.txt + podcast.srt + podcast.vtt

Bounded 26-second chunks

Continuous speech longer than 26 seconds splits at the lowest-energy 100 ms window near the boundary so words stay intact.

Fast silent-gap skipping

In long recordings with sparse speech, silent stretches are skipped in memory while preserving exact start_ms offsets up to 01:00:00+.

Verified on 10s, 5m, and 1h benchmarks
